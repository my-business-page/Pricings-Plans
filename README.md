# Mad Web Studios — Pricing & Plans

One-page Singapore pricing showcase for Mad Web Studios.

## Packages
- Starter — S$599 — 6 months free maintenance — 15 complimentary change chances
- Business — S$999 — 6 months free maintenance — 15 complimentary change chances
- Premium — S$1,499 — 12 months free maintenance — 15 complimentary change chances
- Signature — S$2,499+ — 12 months free maintenance — 15 complimentary change chances

## Maintenance
Includes minor text changes, image replacements, basic content updates, bug/error fixes, broken-link fixes, basic security/compatibility updates, small layout adjustments, and website functionality monitoring.

## Complimentary changes
The 15 requests can be used for completely new pages, major redesigns, new complex features, ecommerce/booking additions, or large amounts of new content. Each eligible request uses one chance.

## Hosting & domain
Websites are hosted on Netlify. Domain fees and renewal fees are separate and depend on the customer's chosen domain/provider.

## Tech
Semantic HTML, CSS, vanilla JavaScript, GSAP + ScrollTrigger, Lenis, responsive design, and no backend.
